# Voice and Tone

## Voice
Clear, calm, helpful. We sound like a knowledgeable colleague, not a manual and not a marketer.

## Rules
- **Say what happened and what to do.** “The settings file is damaged and can’t be read.” + an action.
  Never “Error 2”.
- **No blame.** “Choose between 1 and 12 processors.”, not “Invalid CPU count.”
- **Plain words.** “Shared with Mac” instead of “NAT”. Technical terms appear in Advanced settings and docs.
- **Short.** One idea per sentence; labels are nouns, buttons are verbs.
- **Consistent terms:** *virtual machine* (not VM in the UI), *shut down*, *suspend*, *shared folder*,
  *Move to Trash*.

## English
- Title Case for window titles, buttons and menu items; sentence case for messages.
- Ellipsis (…) on commands that need more input (“New Virtual Machine…”).
- Typographic quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”).

## German
- Informal **du**, as in current Apple German UI.
- Sentence case everywhere except nouns.
- Ellipsis with a space before it: „Neue virtuelle Maschine …“
- German quotes „…“, en dash with spaces – for parentheticals.
- German strings are ~30 % longer: layouts must not truncate (check every UI PR in German).
